# Creative brief: decide the story before cutting

A senior editor never starts on the timeline. Fill `plan.brief` first; judge every later decision
against it ("does this serve the message?").

## The five lines
| Field | Question | Good example |
|---|---|---|
| `message` | What is this video about, in one sentence? | "Failing fast taught me more than any success." |
| `audience` | Who watches, and what do they already know? | "Early founders, no finance background" |
| `arc` | What should they feel, in order? | "shock -> curiosity -> 3 practical lessons -> hope" |
| `hook` | The single strongest line (quote it + S-id) | "S12: 'I lost everything in six months...'" |
| `cta` | What should they do at the end? | "Follow for part 2" / none |
| `target_seconds` | Length you are cutting to | 45 |

## Choosing the hook
Score candidate sentences 1-5 on each, pick the highest total:
1. **Pattern break** - surprising, counter-intuitive, or high stakes ("I lost everything").
2. **Specificity** - numbers, names, concrete images beat abstractions ("300% in 6 months").
3. **Open loop** - raises a question only the video answers.
4. **Delivery** - `energy_vs_median_db` above +2 dB, clean (no fillers), fast-ish wpm.
5. **Standalone** - makes sense without context (no "and so that's why...").

Hook modes: `move` (lift the line to the start, best for Shorts), `tease` (flash it first and keep it
in place, best for longer videos where the line is also the payoff).

## Structures by video type
- **Short (15-60s)**: hook (0-3s) -> context in one line -> 1-3 beats of value -> payoff/twist -> CTA or loop back to the hook.
- **YouTube talking head (5-15 min)**: cold-open hook -> promise ("by the end you'll know...") -> sections, each with its own mini-hook -> payoff -> CTA. Re-hook every 60-90s.
- **Tutorial**: result first (show the finished thing) -> steps (numbered list graphic) -> common mistakes -> recap.
- **Interview / podcast clip**: strongest exchange first, keep question + answer intact, end on the punchline or a laugh.
- **Story / vlog**: setup -> rising stakes -> turning point -> resolution; music carries the arc.
- **Ad / promo (15-30s)**: problem (0-3s) -> product as the answer -> proof (number/testimonial) -> offer + CTA.

## Selecting what to keep
- Cut anything that does not serve the message, even if it is good. Most edits cut 30-70%.
- Keep a question with its answer and a setup with its punchline.
- Prefer the take with fewer fillers and more energy when a line is repeated (compare S-ids with similar text).
- Respect the user's must-keep moments and saved `preferences.json`.
- Length guidance: Shorts/Reels/TikTok 20-45s ideal (max 60/90/180s); YouTube: as long as it stays interesting.
